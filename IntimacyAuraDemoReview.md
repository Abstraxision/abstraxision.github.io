## User Experience Audit

The usability and website performance are functional. Howvever, the mobile UX is not correcntly configured to the screen dimensions. Each subsequent question is out of the frame and the user has to scroll up to read. Some of the newly loaded screens also change in dimension and are shifted to the side or zoomed. For example, the style elements at the start page with the keyhole do not extend past a certain point and the visual interface is not continuous. A white background shows up when scrolling all the way down instead of the gradient pink background. 

### Answer Choices and Button Components
The interactive elements such as navigation and answer selection are also not consistent. Most of the answer choices are categorical and distinct numbers such as 1,2 and ,3 or text based answers. The scale of 1-100 for one answer makes it somewhat confusing to interpret the best way to measure personal responses. A changing range of 1-3 then 1-10 makes it more confusing to answer. Some of the multiple choices especially the non-applicable options at the end of the quiz add more confusion on whether or not certain selections or phrasing is mutually exclusive or repetitive. 

## Key Issue

### Accessibility
A significant issue with the application is the accessibility. Some of the pages are visually difficult to read. The font and graphics, especially the color contrast, are challenging to see on a lot of the pages. The navigation after loading the next question page is challenging, especially on the results page. Other web accessiblity requirements such as alt texts, ARIA labels, readablity and screen reader compatibility standards would also need to be thouroughly audited to comply with the new WCAG guidelines released in 2026.
